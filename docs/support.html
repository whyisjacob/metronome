HERE TO HELP

Keep the music going.

For help with Maelzel, report a bug or request a feature through our support tracker. Include your iPhone or Watch model, OS version, app build, and what happened. Avoid posting private information—the tracker is public.

Connect your Apple Watch

Install Maelzel on your paired Apple Watch and open it on both devices. No code or separate account is needed. The watch follows the phone’s metronome settings or selected song. Start on the watch hands playback over from the phone.

Vibration and screen sleep

The Watch app is vibration-only. Vibration pauses when the watch sleeps or you leave Maelzel. For longer wake time, open Watch Settings → Display & Brightness → Wake Duration → Wake for 70 Seconds. This extends wake time; it does not keep the watch awake indefinitely.

Settings changed on the phone?

If the watch is already playing, tap Stop, then Start to apply the updated settings. Subdivisions and song sections are included.

One purchase. Everything included.

No subscriptions, in-app purchases, consumables, or paid feature unlocks will be added to this app. Purchases and refunds are managed by Apple through your Apple Account.
